Manufacturing Facility

Unit 02 • CNC Machining

CNC Turning Center

Slant-bed precision CNC turning centers engineered for close-tolerance cylindrical turning and threading.

Tolerance: 0.01mm

CNC VERTICAL MILLING MACHINE

Computer numerical controlled milling for complex multi-axis milling operations down to 0.01mm tolerance.

Tolerance: ±0.01mm | Multi-Axis

Custom dedicated multi-station machining for high-volume automated drilling, tapping, and porting.

Capability: Multi-Spindle SPM

Heavy-duty hydraulic press for post-forging component straightening, coining, and dimensional calibration.

Operation: Straightening & Coining

Band Saw Cutting Machine

High-speed automatic band sawing machine for clean, perpendicular raw material billet preparation.
