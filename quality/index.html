Quality Control Protocol

3-Stage Comprehensive Inspection

A rigorous quality framework ensuring zero-defect manufacturing from incoming material to final shipment.

STAGE 01

Raw Material Inspection

Verification of incoming certified steel bars, optical emission spectrometry for chemical composition, ultrasonic testing for internal flaws, and hardness testing prior to shearing.

STAGE 02

In-Process Monitoring

First-piece dimensional approval before production run, statistical process control (SPC), regular CNC tool offset verification, and 10-micron dimensional audits during active machining cycles.

STAGE 03

Final Dimensional & PPAP Audit

100% gauge verification of critical dimensions down to 0.01mm, surface roughness profilometry (Ra measurement), Level 3 PPAP dossier sign-off, and certified test certificate generation.
