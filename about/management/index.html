Standard Operating Procedure

Our Organization & Management Flow

A disciplined 12-stage operational pipeline engineered to guarantee traceability, dimensional precision, and timely delivery from initial RFQ through to final dispatch.

Step 01 →

Enquiry / RFQ

Receipt of customer drawings, component specifications, target volumes, and delivery timeline requirements.

Step 02 →

Feasibility Study

Engineering evaluation of metallurgical grades, forging press tonnage, tooling design, and CNC cycle times.

Step 03 →

Quotation & Costing

Transparent commercial proposal submitted within 24–48 hours detailing unit pricing, lead time, and tooling investment.

Step 04 →

Purchase Order

Formal commercial agreement and purchase order acceptance locking technical parameters and delivery milestones.

Step 05 →

Sales Order Confirmation

Internal work order creation and job card release logged directly into production scheduling systems.

Step 06 →

Drawing / Design Review

Detailed CAD/CAM review, forging die tooling approval, fixture design, and CNC program validation.

Step 07 →

Production Planning

Certified raw billet procurement, band saw cutting allocation, and press line scheduling.

Step 08 →

Tool & Gauge Readiness

Preparation and calibration verification of custom gauges, clamping fixtures, and cutting inserts.

Step 09 →

Manufacturing Process

Hot friction screw press forging followed by high-accuracy CNC turning, vertical milling, and SPM operations.

Step 10 →

Finishing / Surface Treatment

Hydraulic thread rolling, ultrasonic deburring, industrial dot matrix or laser marking, and surface protection.

Step 11 →

Final Quality Inspection

100% dimensional inspection, surface roughness audit, Level 3 PPAP verification, and test certification.

Step 12 ✓

Packing & Dispatch

Anti-corrosion oiling, VCI vacuum wrapping, heavy-duty wooden box crating, and on-schedule freight dispatch.
