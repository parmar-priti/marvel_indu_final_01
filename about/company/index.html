Our Story

Bridging Metallurgical Heritage with Modern CNC Machining

Founded in 2024, Marvel Techno Industries represents a premier industrial benchmark for high-precision hot forged and CNC machined components, operating from an expansive 23,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility.

Our directors bring a rich 20+ year industrial pedigree rooted in Maruti Techno Industries (established in 2004). With deep metallurgical knowledge, friction screw press forging, and high-accuracy multi-axis CNC machining, we serve as a single-source manufacturing partner for critical engineering sectors worldwide.

Under our governing philosophy “Discipline Delivers Quality”, every stage of our workflow — from raw billet chemistry testing to finish turning down to 0.01mm tolerance — is stringently documented and verified.
